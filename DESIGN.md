# Terms: UI direction ("Terms Cards")

**References** (principles only, nothing copied):
- [teampot.xyz](https://teampot.xyz): restraint, large light numerals with quiet grey cents, one dark money card per screen, pill controls.
- [monk.com](https://monk.com): accounts-receivable niche leader; the warmth of a finance product that talks plainly.
- [ramp.com](https://ramp.com) and [mercury.com](https://mercury.com): calm surfaces, numbers first.

**Own metaphor: credit as an object.**
- Every customer's credit line is a premium card, and its finish *is* the decision:
  - coral: cut
  - green: raised
  - iridescent: needs approval
  - amber: hold or prepay
  - ivory: keep
- The hero is a dark "credit book" card (total limits, grey cents) beside a fanned wallet of this week's cards.
- Tapping a card opens its **signed term sheet**: limit, terms, each order's fate, the clauses that applied, facts from the SEC filings, the model's reasoning, the Zetaris evidence, and a signature line.
- When the change needs the credit manager, the signature block *is* the approval control (Approve and sign / Reject), and the seal changes from PENDING to EXECUTED or REJECTED.
- The agents work behind the cards. They show up in the desk log, not as a cast.

**Type:** Host Grotesk at 300 for numerals and 500 for headings, with tight tracking; Fragment Mono for labels, tickers and query ids.

**Shape:** 20px card radius, 26–30px panels, 999px pill buttons.

**Live:** the cards under review shimmer; the book card's line streams what the desk is doing; decisions land on the cards as the Executor applies them.
